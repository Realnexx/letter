# Design
Mode: Experience. The supplied master skill is the visual authority.

Warm ivory #F5F0E7 is the dominant ground; deep burgundy #55333C is the ink and physical envelope; champagne gold #C5A15C is reserved for the wax seal and fine accents. Cormorant Garamond carries editorial headings and letters, DM Sans carries controls, Allura carries greetings and signatures. Fonts ship locally.

The first screen centers an actual layered envelope below a two-line editorial greeting. Opening uses a GSAP timeline: seal release, flap rotation at its top edge, paper extraction and unfold, then reading. The reading surface is a quiet paper sheet with a desktop chapter rail and a compact mobile toolbar. Five chapters use botanical, appreciation-note, memory, wish and intimate treatments while retaining typography and spacing. Decoration never claims to be a personal photograph.

Tokens: page background #F5F0E7; paper #FFFCF5; ink #55333C; muted ink #78676A; accent #C5A15C; divider #55333C25. Body text uses 21px serif with 1.6 line height; controls use 9–12px DM Sans. Touch buttons retain generous padding; keyboard focus uses burgundy outlines. Reduced motion disables effects and keeps all content readable.

# Product
A Letter for Her is a private-feeling, publicly hostable static birthday letter experience. The visitor opens a sealed envelope and reads exactly five configurable chapters, with optional supplied narration and a replayable finale. Personal facts, memories, photos and recordings must come from the owner. Sample content remains clearly identified until replaced. A small Vercel endpoint optionally sends anonymous visit notices and consented birthday replies through Resend. There is no database or visitor authentication. The email integration remains disabled until server-side delivery credentials are configured.
